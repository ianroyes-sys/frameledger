# Naming exploration

Researched 28 September 2026. Recommendation only; the application still uses FrameLedger.

## Recommendation: Earnframe

**Earnframe — Money in focus.**

Descriptor: **The financial workspace for independent creatives.**

Earnframe connects the money a creator earns with the context of the work that earned it. It is easy to pronounce (earn-frame), uses familiar spelling, works for a photographer or an agency, and gives the existing product a warmer identity. It can extend to invoices, cards, project wallets, and intelligent cash-flow tools without putting AI or a particular provider in the name.

Positioning: Run the financial side of your creative business from one place.

Supporting message: Get paid. Give every dollar a job. See what your work earned.

Potential weakness: “Earn” can resemble income-generation or rewards products. Lead with the creative-business descriptor and real workflow screenshots; avoid guaranteed-earnings, interest, or yield language.

## Brand direction for Earnframe

- Wordmark: simple mixed-case Earnframe, generous spacing, a distinctive but readable “E.” Avoid dollar signs or a bank-building symbol.
- Symbol: two open framing corners, suggesting that the product brings the financial picture into view. Avoid a camera illustration that limits the future audience.
- Voice: calm, direct, precise. “You have $470 available after reserves and commitments.”
- Palette: retain the existing restrained financial workspace and its established accent until the name is settled. A naming change alone does not justify rebuilding working screens.
- Feature names remain literal: Project Wallets, Safe to Spend, Money Autopilot, Purchase Simulator. The product should be easy to understand before the user learns brand vocabulary.
- Use “financial platform” or “financial workspace.” Do not append “Bank” or imply that the software company is a chartered institution. Provider-approved disclosures must describe the actual services and legal entities.

## Before a public rename

1. Perform professional clearance for the name and confusingly similar spellings/sounds in the intended countries and software/financial services categories. Final legal name clearance is still required.
2. Obtain current registrar results and direct social-handle checks; no purchase was made during this task.
3. Check pronunciation and recall with a small group of photographers, designers, and agency owners. Ask them what they think the product does from the name and descriptor.
4. Confirm provider-approved company, card, statement, and disclosure naming requirements before printing or issuing anything.
5. Apply the selected brand through a central configuration, app metadata, payment-page branding, email templates, legal pages, and eventual mobile assets. Preserve database identifiers and existing customer links through the rename.
